Focus Quest privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Focus Quest is a focus timer wrapped in a small woodcutting game. It's built to work without an account and without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Focus Quest has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers. We don't run a server that receives your data.

What's stored on your device

Your progress (focus trips, levels, items, your character's look and name) and a few settings are saved only on your device: in your browser's storage for the web version, or in the app's private storage on Android and desktop. You can download a copy of your save from Settings and delete it by clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your own device to tell you when a trip or break ends. Nothing is sent to us.

Feedback

The "Send feedback" button opens a page on GitHub. Anything you post there is handled under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases and ads

Focus Quest currently has no ads and no purchases. If that changes (for example an optional supporter unlock, or ads outside of focus trips), this page will be updated first to explain what the ad or payment provider collects.

Children

Focus Quest doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
